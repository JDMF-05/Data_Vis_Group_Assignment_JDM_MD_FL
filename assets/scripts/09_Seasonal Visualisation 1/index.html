    This Heat Map addresses two questions: 
How do Genres vary by season?
Which are the most listened genres by season?
    Our objective is to identify genres that show significant changes in listening activity depending on the time of year. 


Results and Discussion: What the Data Actually Shows

            The Heat Map shoSws that some genres are much more dependent on season than others. It is important to remember that the data is scaled: a genre reaching its maximum does not necessarily make it the most popular genre overall. Instead, the graph shows when each genre performs particularly strongly relative to itself. The clearest example is Holiday & Seasonal, which reaches its maximum during Winter. Unlike a normal hit, a Christmas song can disappear for most of the year and then return every December. The calendar therefore temporarily reverses the normal lifecycle of a song, allowing decades old recordings to compete again with modern releases.

    Winter also shows strong relative values for Jazz & Blues, Metal, Classical & Orchestral and Soundtrack & Stage, but overall contains fewer strongly represented genres. 

    Spring introduces a wider mixture, including Folk / Acoustic / Roots, Metal, Hip-Hop & Rap, Pop and R&B / Soul / Funk. 

    The greatest variety, however, appears during Summer. Caribbean & Afro-Caribbean, Latin & Regional Mexican, Electronic & Dance, Country & Americana, Jazz & Blues and Pop all show strong relative values.

    One possible explanation is the increase in holidays, festivals, nightlife and outdoor events. Summer festivals in particular expose audiences to several genres in the same environment, potentially encouraging more varied listening. The strong presence of Caribbean and Latin genres also creates an interesting connection between the seasonal and geographical parts of our story.

    Finally, Fall shifts toward African Contemporary & Diaspora, Caribbean & Afro-Caribbean, Pop, Rock & Alternative and Electronic & Dance.

    The overall pattern suggests that musical behaviour responds not only to historical trends, but also to the activities and traditions associated with different times of the year.
